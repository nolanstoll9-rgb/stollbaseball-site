Questions

FAQ

When and where is camp?

Saturday, November 28, from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM at Old Orchard Park. Please arrive about 10 minutes early to check in.

How much does it cost?

$75 if you register by Sunday, November 15. After that, it's $90.

How do I register?

Tap any Register button to pay on Venmo (@nolanstoll09). Put the player's name and age in the payment note. Once the payment goes through, the spot is saved.

What should my player bring?

A glove, a bat, a helmet, cleats or turf shoes, a water bottle, and a snack. Catchers should bring their gear if they have it.

What ages and skill levels is camp for?

Players of all levels are welcome. Coaching is adjusted to each player. If you have a question about your player, message Nolan on Instagram.

What is SBC Academy? Do I need it for camp?

No, camp is separate. SBC Academy is an optional monthly online membership with weekly training videos in a private Discord. It's a good way to keep working on what you learn at camp.
